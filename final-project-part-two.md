## Target audience
<!--

Audience: African Legislators, NGO Leadership

I chose this audience because these are the type of people who can make the biggest immediate difference. Niger was only as successful in its recovery of the West African giraffe because of legislation against poaching as well as enforcement of that legislation, a combination effort between African policy-makers and NGOs. Even further than that, there were  also public education campaigns organized. Reinforcing the urgency of this matter to these two groups of people is the most effective means by which to see proper, widespread change regarding this issue.
